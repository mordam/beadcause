# v81 — a relay trail with no stylesheet under it (bc-bmry.4)

`public/app.js` gains two renderers on the epic card: `p0RelayHtml`, a status line on each
row of an epic's tree saying where a department relay has got to, and `p0RelayTrailHtml`,
the whole trail on the bead you open. Between them they draw seven classes that did not
exist at v80 — `.p0-relay`, `.p0-relay-sum`, `.p0-trail`, `.p0-trail-row`, `.p0-trail-who`,
`.p0-trail-when`, `.p0-trail-note`, `.p0-trail-flag` — and `public/style.css` gains the
rules for all of them in the same commit.

**A v80 stylesheet under a v81 `app.js` is the pair that forces the number, and both halves
of it are the same failure: unstyled text that still reads as a sentence.** The row's
`.p0-relay` is `flex: 1 1 100%` in a wrapping flex row, and without that rule it is an
inline span at body size sitting between a bead's id and its title — so `⇄ clio · check · 2
steps · 40m ago` is drawn *inside* the row's own text, at the same weight as the title,
sixty times down an epic's tree. Nothing errors and nothing is missing; the tree simply
becomes unreadable in the one place it is read fastest. The trail below is milder and the
same shape: `.p0-trail-note` and `.p0-trail-flag` are what put the sentence and the flag on
their own lines, and without them a step's note wraps into the column of the three marks
before it and reads as a caption on the timestamp rather than as what the role did.

The reverse pair — a v81 stylesheet under a v80 `app.js` — draws nothing at all, because no
v80 page ever emits any of these classes. That direction is merely older, which is the
direction this repo is happy to leave alone.

The server half forces nothing on its own. `/api/bead` gains `relay` and each tree row on
the epic card gains `relay`, and both are `null` for every bead no relay has ever run
on — which is every bead in every workspace but deluvia's studio. A page cached from a
newer deploy against an older daemon draws nothing for the missing field, which is the case
both renderers are written for and what `test/relayjournal.mjs` asserts first: a bead
nothing relayed draws the empty string, not an empty box.

## The number

Written against v80 and expected to land as v81. If it collides — see the
[README](../../README.md)'s renumber procedure — take both notes, move this one to the next
free number, and set the `const` in `public/sw.js` by hand to match the highest note in this
directory. Two branches writing the *same* value to that `const` merge clean and silently,
and the note's filename is the only thing that reports it.
