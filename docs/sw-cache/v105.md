# v105 — the bead detail sheet grows a ⋮, and an edit card behind it (bc-ka5y.43)

The graph's detail sheet had no menu. Everything it offered was a row it had decided to
draw — the owner buttons, the adopt picker on an orphan — so an action that is not one of
those was not reachable from the phone at all. It has a ⋮ now, the inbox card's, with one
item behind it: **Edit**, which swaps the sheet body for the six-field card `/endorse`
already uses and posts it to `/api/bead/edit` (bc-ka5y.41).

| half | what is new |
|---|---|
| `public/graph.html` | a `.menu-wrap` in `.sheet-actions`, holding `#sheet-menu.kebab` |
| `public/graph.js` | `sheetMenuHtml` draws `.menu-why`; `sheetEditHtml` draws `.edit-err` |
| `public/style.css` | those two rules, plus `.sheet-head .kebab` sizing the ⋮ to its neighbours |

**Two of those three pairings are why this version exists**, and the third is the loud
one. A phone holding an old `style.css` under the new `graph.js` gets `.menu-why` and
`.edit-err` as unstyled body text — the first is a greyed-out item's explanation, which
unstyled reads as a second menu item you cannot tap; the second is a *refusal*, which
unstyled loses the only thing that marks it as one and reads as a caption under the form
that just failed to save. Both are the "looks like a working page" failure. The third
pairing is `.sheet-head .kebab`: without the new rule the ⋮ falls back to its native 48px
circle beside two 40px rounded squares, which is ugly rather than misleading, and is the
half a cached-CSS phone would notice first.

The other direction is harmless: an old `graph.html` cached without the wrap gives a
sheet with no ⋮ at all, which is the sheet as it was, and `$('sheet-menu')` is guarded so
the page still loads. That is deliberate but not sufficient — a page that loads and
quietly has no editor is exactly what the version is for.

No server half. `/api/bead/edit` landed under bc-ka5y.41 with nothing consuming it, and
this is the client that does; `/api/bead` is unchanged and is what the sheet repaints
from after a save.
