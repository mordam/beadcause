# v108 — adding a bead-space ends by asking which of its epics are yours (bc-9i62w)

＋ Add a bead-space gains a third round. Once a bead-space is created or linked, the dialog
lists that bead-space's open epics and P0s and asks which of them to own and which to put an
Epic Advocate on — zero or more.

| half | what is new |
|---|---|
| `public/addspace.js` | `done()` hands off to `paintOwnership()`, which reads `GET /api/workspaces/roots` and posts `action: 'own'`; `paintDone()` is the old closing screen |
| `public/style.css` | `.addspace-roots`, `.addspace-root`, `.addspace-root-what`, `.addspace-root-meta`, `.addspace-root-picks`, `.addspace-root-pick` |

**The mixed pair that breaks is v107's `style.css` under the new `addspace.js`.** The new
round draws a list of epics, each with two checkboxes, into a dialog whose stylesheet has
never heard of any of it. With no `max-height` on the list, a linked work tracker offering
forty epics pushes the only button below the bottom of a phone's dialog. On a phone, the
round you cannot finish is the round with no way out.

The other way round is merely older. v107's `addspace.js` under the new stylesheet never
draws the classes, and the dialog closes on "Added" as it always did.

**`SHELL` is unchanged.** No file is added or renamed, so there is nothing new to precache.
