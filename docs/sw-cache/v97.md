# v97 — the advocate console becomes the Advocates pane, chip row and all (bc-khoe.4)

`public/index.html`'s Advocates container was an empty `data-pending` box holding a place
in the grammar; this fills it with the whole of `monitor.html` below its top bar — the chip
row and all three of its sections. Tapping the Advocates pill from Home is a `display:none`
swap now rather than a document load, and it was the last of the four containers, so no
pane in this document is pending any more.

`monitor.html` is **untouched as an address**. `/monitor`, `/advocates`, `/monitor.html`,
`/sessions`, `/work`, `/work.html`, `/prs`, `/pulls` and `/prs.html` all still answer with
that document, because several of them are on a phone's home screen and in the
notifications the ship path sends. Landing them on the pane is bc-khoe.30.7. So all four of
`montabs.js`, `monitor.js`, `prs.js` and `mirror.js` now run in **two** documents and ask
which one they are in — `panes.has('advocates')`, of the document rather than of a flag.

**No element id moved, which is the one way this is a smaller bump than v96's.** The row and
its sections were already called `mon-tabs`, `mon`, `prs` and `mirror`, none of which is the
`#advocates` hash that names this view, so there is no fragment target to dodge. `observing`
kept its bare name because v96 renamed the release board's to `rel-observing` for exactly
this collision. What did move is where two elements sit: `⦿ observing` and the roster's
tally came down out of the top bar into the right-hand end of the chip row, because the
shell's bar belongs to every view and those two are facts about one of them.

**What makes it a bump is the wiring, and one half of it is genuinely broken rather than
merely older.** In the shell these three sections must not open sockets of their own:
`public/panestage.js` holds the document's one poll and fans its answers out, and
`public/montabs.js` is the pane's single registration with it.

| the phone holds | against | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| v96 `monitor.js` / `prs.js` / `mirror.js` (each mounts its own `stream.follow`) | v97 `index.html` (the pane markup is there, so their elements resolve) | **broken** — three extra parked `/api/poll` requests from one screen beside the one the stager already holds, each a `bd` sweep per event on the daemon, and the board's is a `gh` sweep per repo on every merge behind two hidings. Nothing throws and nothing looks wrong, which is what makes it worth a version rather than a comment |
| v96 `monitor.js` (writes `busy` on `#pulse`) | v97 `index.html` | **broken**, quietly: the brand dot is the whole document's and `public/report.js` drives it off its own count of what is in flight, so a second writer clears it under a fetch of the inbox's that is still out |
| v96 `montabs.js` (no `up()`, no `onWake`, no `stage.register`) | v97 `monitor.js` / `prs.js` | survivable: both reach for `up()` with `?.` and fall back to their own `hidden` attribute, and the row builds itself on `DOMContentLoaded` as it always did. The pane works and stands down one hiding late |
| v96 `index.html` (pane still `data-pending`, none of the four scripts loaded) | v97 anything | survivable and correct: `panes.has('advocates')` is false, so the pill stays the plain `<a href="/monitor">` it was and that document answers it. This is the app exactly as v96 shipped |

The two broken rows are the same pairing from either end, and both are cured by the version
moving as one — which is what a cache version is for. `SHELL` itself does not change: all
four scripts and all nine of that page's paths were already in it, and they stay until
bc-khoe.30.7 turns those paths into redirects, which is the commit that owes taking them
out. `public/mirror.js` is still deliberately **not** in it: the Mirror follows another
device, so a cached copy would buy a working chip over a list that can never fill.
