# v58 — the epic board's heading became a control (bc-eevn)

The section of epics above the inbox list folds now, and it is called "Epics assigned to
you" rather than "Your P0s". Two files move together: `public/app.js` draws the heading as
a `<button data-act="p0-fold">` with a `.chev` and a count, and reads `state.p0shut` to
decide whether the cards come with it; `public/style.css` turns `.p0-kind` from an 11px
label into a full-width `var(--tap)` row and adds `.p0-kind-n`.

**A v57 stylesheet beside a v58 app.js is the pair that matters, and it is the quiet
one.** `.p0-kind` at v57 has no `display: flex`, no width and no height — so the heading
renders as a browser-default button: a small grey chrome-styled box, boxed and centred,
with the count jammed against the label instead of at the far end, and the chevron sitting
inline pointing right whether the board is open or shut. Nothing throws, the fold still
works, and the one thing on screen that tells you which state you are in — the chevron
turning — is the thing that stops. A board that is folded therefore looks exactly like a
board with a stray button over it, which is a bug report about the epics having vanished.

The other way round is harmless: a v58 stylesheet over a v57 app.js styles a `<div>` that
has no chevron and no count, and gets a taller heading than it needs.

Nothing else on the page is affected — `state.p0shut` is display only, `underOwnedP0s`
never reads it, and the list under the board is narrowed to your epics' descendants
whether the board is on screen or not.

The fetch handler is network-first, so this pair only exists on an offline or flapping
load and heals on the next online one. Bumped for the usual reason: the cheap half is a
stale stylesheet, and a missing stylesheet is not loud.
