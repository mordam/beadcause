# v79 — ＋ on My Epics starts an epic, and the picker it opens is new markup (bc-khoe.27.2)

The board's own `＋ Start an epic` button, at the foot of the cards, is gone. On `My Epics`
the floating ＋ *is* that create: it opens a panel of the roots you own that are not
started, and choosing one puts it on the board. `public/inboxfilter.js`'s `compose` flag
stops being a boolean and becomes the word for what ＋ makes here (`epic`, `chat`), with a
`creates()` beside `composes()`; `public/app.js` branches the one click listener on that
word, keeps `p0CandsHtml` for the rows and `setOnBoard` for the write both controls share,
and drops `state.p0picker` with the section that read it; `public/index.html` gains a
second panel, `#compose-epics`; `public/style.css` gains the rules that give it a width and
a scroll, and loses `.p0-pick` and `.p0-board.bare`.

**The pair that must not be mixed is `index.html` and `style.css`.** A v79 document carries
`<div id="compose-epics" class="compose-pick ws-pick epics">`, and a v78 stylesheet has
never heard of `.epics` — so the panel inherits `.compose-pick`'s `width: max-content`,
which for a column of two-line candidate cards means "as wide as the longest bead title"
and no scroll cap at all. It lays out: a panel running off the side of the phone and, with
a dozen candidates, off the top of it — anchored to the bottom corner, an unbounded panel
grows *upward*, so the rows that leave the screen are the ones nothing can scroll back to.
A working-looking page that cannot be used, which is what the version exists to prevent.

**Both script directions are merely older.** A v78 `app.js` under a v79 document never asks
`creates()` and never looks for `#compose-epics`: ＋ starts a chat on every kind that has
one, the new panel sits hidden and unreferenced, and the board — drawn by that same older
`app.js` — still carries its own `＋ Start an epic` at the foot. Two ways to do it rather
than none. A v79 `app.js` under a v78 document finds no `#compose-epics`, and every reach
for it is guarded (`composeEpicsEl` is null-checked in `hideComposePick`, `showEpicPick`
returns early, the row listener is `?.`) — so ＋ on My Epics does nothing at all when
tapped, which is the one loss, and the board's picker is gone with the new `app.js`. That
direction is why the guards are there rather than being defensive habit: it is a real load,
for one refresh, on a phone that has the document cached.

`public/inboxfilter.js` is the third file and it cannot break either half. Older `app.js`
never calls `creates()`; newer `app.js` calls it optionally and falls back to `chat`, which
is what ＋ did on every kind before any of this.

`test/p0start.mjs`, `test/p0card.mjs`, `test/p0bead.mjs`, `test/composekind.mjs` and
`scripts/p0start-check.mjs` moved with it; none of those is on a phone.
