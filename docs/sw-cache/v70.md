# v70 — starting an epic from the board (bc-s8mc)

The P0 board grows a picker at its foot — `＋ Start an epic`, opening a list of the P0s you
own that are open and have not been started — and each card grows `↩ Take it off the board`.
`public/app.js` draws both; `public/style.css` styles them; the two writes behind them
(`POST /api/bead/start`, `POST /api/bead/unstart`) are server-side and cache nothing.

**The pair is broken rather than merely older, and the direction that bites is the usual
one.** A v70 `app.js` under a v69 stylesheet has rules for none of `.p0-pick`, `.p0-picker`,
`.p0-cand`, `.p0-cand-head`, `.p0-cand-title`, `.p0-off` or `.p0-board.bare`. What that
draws is a full-width unstyled button under the cards and, once tapped, a column of
unstyled buttons — each carrying an id, a count and a title with no box, no spacing and no
target size, run together with the cards above them. It is tappable and it does the right
thing, which is the failure mode this number exists for: a screen that looks like the board
half-loaded rather than like a control that is not there.

The other direction is the mild one. A v69 `app.js` under the v70 stylesheet draws no picker
at all and the new rules match nothing — the board as it was on 2026-08-16, which is a page
that is simply older. `.p0-acts` gains `flex-wrap: wrap` in v70, and a v69 page's two-control
acts row is unaffected by being allowed to wrap when it does not need to.

## What moved

- `public/app.js`: `p0PickerHtml`, the `p0-pick` / `p0-start` / `p0-unstart` handlers,
  `state.p0picker`, the `↩ Take it off the board` button in `.p0-acts`, and the bare-offer
  branch of `p0SectionHtml` for a board with no cards on it yet.
- `public/style.css`: `.p0-pick`, `.p0-picker`, `.p0-cand`, `.p0-cand-head`, `.p0-cand-title`,
  `.p0-off`, `.p0-board.bare`, and `flex-wrap: wrap` on the existing `.p0-acts`.
- No file joins or leaves `SHELL`, so nothing here is a precache list change.
