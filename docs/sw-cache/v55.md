# v55 — both cards say which model a bead runs on, and the stylesheet grows their rules (bc-nc6o.4/.5)

`public/app.js` draws a `🧠 sonnet · low` chip in the inbox card's meta row, `public/graph.js`
draws a `Model` row on the bead sheet under the session link, and `public/style.css` gains
the rules that lay both of them out.

## Why this one is a bump rather than an additive skip

It is the named mixed pair twice over, and `test/swbump.mjs` says so by name: seven classes
(`.model-row`, `.model-kind`, `.model-picked`, `.model-why`, `.model-ran`, `.is-none`/`.is-bad`
on the first of those and `.is-diverged` on the last) are newly drawn by one half of SHELL
and newly styled by the other, plus `.pill.model` and its three states beside them.

The sheet row is the half that decides it. Without its rules `.model-row` is a bare `div`:
four spans of undivided text — `Modelopuscomplexity:highran on opus` — run together on one
line between the session link and the description, where every neighbouring row on that
sheet is a bordered 44px box. It reads as a rendering fault on a page that is otherwise
working, which is exactly the failure the version exists to prevent. The chip is the milder
half of the same thing: `.pill.model` is drawn as an outline where the cached stylesheet
only knows `.pill` as a filled one, so `opus` and `sonnet` arrive uppercased in a row of
identical grey pills and the outline-versus-filled distinction that says *plan* from *fact*
is gone entirely.

The fetch handler is network-first, so the window is an offline or flapping load; that
narrows it and does not excuse it.

## What is in it

- `public/app.js` — `modelChipHtml`, and its call in `cardHtml`'s meta row beside the
  workspace, the id and the priority.
- `public/graph.js` — `modelRowHtml`, and its call in `sheetHtml` directly under
  `sessionRowHtml`.
- `public/style.css` — `.pill.model` (with `.ran`, `.diverged`, `.bad`), `.model-row`,
  `.model-kind`, `.model-picked`, `.model-why` (with `.is-none`, `.is-bad`), `.model-ran`
  (with `.is-diverged`).

## What is *not* a reason for it

The derivation is not. `lib/modelcard.js` is on the daemon and `lib/` is not in SHELL; the
new `model` field on `/api/questions` and `/api/bead` is an addition to a payload, and a
cached page that has never heard of it draws nothing — both renderers return `''` for a
bead carrying no `model`, which is asserted in `test/modelcard.mjs` for precisely this
case. A page from the future against a daemon from the past is the shape that costs
nothing here; it is the two *files* that must not be mixed.
