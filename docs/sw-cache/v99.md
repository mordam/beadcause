# v99 — the endorse row says what was learned after the bead was filed (bc-xl7n.76.2)

The endorsement queue is the fattest list in the app on purpose, and every field it draws
is the **filing agent's own words**, written at the moment it found the work and before
anybody had looked at it. The evidence that a bead should *not* be endorsed is later than
that by definition, and it arrives in the two places the row did not read: the bead's
comment thread, and a separate `human` bead asking about it.

Measured on 2026-08-20 — bc-wi3s had been finished work for two days, an epic advocate had
written "the suite is green" on it as a comment and filed an open P1 (bc-xl7n.101)
recommending it be closed rather than endorsed. The endorse sweep took it anyway, in a
batch of 56. Nothing on the row said a word about either.

So the folded row gains two lines, and both halves of the shell change together:

| half | what is new |
|---|---|
| `public/endorse.js` | `saidHtml` draws `.eq-last` / `.eq-last-who` / `.eq-last-text`; `questionsHtml` draws `.eq-ask` / `.eq-ask-title` / `.eq-ask-sep` |
| `public/style.css` | the six rules those names need, and nothing else |

**That pairing is the whole reason for this version.** A phone holding either cached half
alone still renders a page — the question line simply arrives as unstyled inline text on a
row whose entire job is to look different from its neighbours, which is exactly the
"looks like a working page" failure the cache version exists to prevent.

**`.eq-last` and not `.eq-said`.** `.eq-said` was already taken, by the sticky line that
says what a verdict just did. `test/css.mjs` refuses a second top-level declaration of a
selector outright, which is how the collision was found rather than shipped.

The server half (`lib/endorsequeue.js`, `lib/openquestion.js`) is additive on the wire:
`latestComment` and `questions` are two new fields on a row, and an older cached
`endorse.js` reading a payload that carries them draws exactly what it drew before.
