# v85 — a new `public/app.js` under an older stylesheet draws the advocate as a wall of run-on text (bc-r2b5.2)

Two shell files change together: `public/app.js` gains the P0 card's four advocate states
and the sheet a tap on the advocate line opens, and `public/style.css` gains the ~20
`.p0-adv-*` / `.p0-done` rules that lay both of them out.

**The pair that matters is the new script under the previous stylesheet**, which is the
direction a cached phone actually lands in: the service worker serves whichever of the two
it happens to be holding, and a repaint after an update can reach the new `app.js` before
the CSS has been refetched.

What survives that, and it is most of it: the sheet is `class="p0-full p0-adv"`, so
`.p0-full`'s `position: fixed; inset: 0; z-index: 40` still applies from the old
stylesheet — it is still a full-screen layer with its own scroller, still has its back
button, and being later in the section's markup than `p0FullHtml` it still paints over the
epic tab at the same z-index. The close offer is still a real `<button>` with its own words
on it. Nothing is unreachable and nothing is missing.

What does not survive is the **layout of everything inside it**. `.p0-adv-facts` is what
makes "Where it is" a label column beside a value column; without it the three facts render
as bare inline spans, so `ASSIGNMENT` runs straight into `recorded on the bead, by the label
the launch stamps` and then straight into `A WINDOW`, with no break between any of them. The
plan's groups lose their boxes and their bead pills lose the fade a closed one has, so "1 of
2 done" is a phrase in a paragraph rather than a count beside a name. And the card's own
control loses `.p0-adv-open`'s column, so `🧭 Advocated` and `last looked 3h ago` become one
run-on line inside a 40px button and the second half is clipped — which is the half the
whole bead is about.

So: readable-with-effort rather than wrong, and unreadable on the one line the board is
scanned for. Worth the version.

**The reverse — a new `style.css` on a phone still holding the old `app.js` — is nothing.**
Every rule added is namespaced `.p0-adv-*` or `.p0-done`, and no page draws those classes
until the new script does. The one selector that is *widened* rather than added
(`.p0-row-id, .p0-adv-kid-id`) leaves the existing half byte-identical.
