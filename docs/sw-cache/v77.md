# v77 — the space's settings become a page, and a v76 shell has a chip over an empty pane (bc-khoe.10)

The selected space's settings left `monitor.html` altogether. They are `/config` now — a
new document (`public/config.html`, `public/config.js`), a new alias pair in `serveStatic`
(`/config` and `/settings`), and the eighth pill on `public/viewbar.js`'s row. The
**Config** chip and its `<section id="config">` are gone from `monitor.html`; `monitor.js`
loses `spaceHtml`, `tri`, `repoTri`, `loadSpace`, `saveSpace` and every `data-space-*`
press branch with them, and draws the roster alone.

**A v76 `monitor.html` under a v77 `monitor.js` is the direction that shows.** That page
still has the Config chip and the empty section behind it, and the new script knows nothing
about either: `public/montabs.js` reads the chips out of the HTML, so the chip still swaps
to a pane holding the "Reading this space…" placeholder it ships with, and nothing ever
fills it. The settings are still reachable — the pill row is drawn by `viewbar.js`, which
is in the shell and updates with it, so **Config** is on the row and `/config` serves. So
the failure is a dead chip beside a live pill, not a lost screen.

**The reverse — a v77 `monitor.html` under a v76 `monitor.js` — is the harmless one.** The
old script looks for `#config`, finds nothing (`const cfg` is null and every write through
it was already guarded), and falls back to drawing the settings card at the top of the
roster where it lived before bc-me2b. That is the app as it was two days ago: correct, and
one screenful of switches above the first advocate.

**The pill row and the new document have to arrive together, which is the real pair.** A
shell holding v76's `viewbar.js` has no Config pill and no `/config` precached, so a phone
on that shell reaches the settings only through whichever `monitor.html` it also has. Both
files are in `SHELL`, so one `install` brings the row, the page and its script at once —
which is the whole reason the four new paths (`/config`, `/settings`, `/config.html`,
`/config.js`) are precached rather than left to network-first.

`lib/presence.js` needs no change: `config` has been one of its `VIEWS` since bc-me2b gave
the chip a `data-view`, and `public/mirror.js` already names it. What changed is only who
reports it — `montabs.js` did, and `config.js` does.
