# v107 — Home and My Epics become two pills (bc-fwp2c)

The pill you land on and the pill the epic board draws on were one pill, and its badge
could only ever be the sum of every kind — which is how `My Epics 46` came to sit over a
tracker holding three epics. They are two now: `home` is the place, `epics` is the board.

| half | what is new |
|---|---|
| `public/inboxfilter.js` | the `home` kind; `epics` gains `test: () => false` and `board: true`; `current()` falls back to `home`; `survey` derives `home` from the slices and takes `epics` from the caller |
| `public/viewbar.js` | the `home` pill, first in `PILLS`; `narrowed` seeds from `home` rather than `route.HOME` |
| `public/app.js` | `boardOnly` drops its `cards > 0` clause, `epicsIsBoard` is gone, `surveyKinds` passes the cards unconditionally, and `epicsEmptyHtml` draws the screen that clause used to protect |

**Both mixed pairs are broken, and each takes a whole screen with it.**

A phone holding **v106's `app.js`** under the new filter asks `current()` and is told
`home`. Every gate in `render` that draws the board is keyed on `view === 'epics'`, so it
answers false on the screen you land on and `p0SectionHtml` is never called: **the epic
board is simply not there**, on the one pill it has ever been drawn on, with no error and
nothing on screen to say a section is missing.

The other way round is the same size. **v106's `inboxfilter.js`** under the new `app.js`
returns `epics` from `current()` on an unnarrowed Home, which the new `boardOnly` now
takes at face value without the `cards > 0` guard that used to soften it — so `listHere`
is false and **the inbox list is never drawn**. You land on a board, or on nothing at all
when no epic is started, and the forty rows that were the point of the page are one pill
away with nothing saying so.

**v106's `viewbar.js` is the quiet third.** It has no `home` in `PILLS`, so the new
filter's `home` matches nothing in the row and `aria-current` lands on no pill at all: the
row goes on working, every pill tappable, and none of them says where you are. Under the
new `viewbar.js` with the old filter the mirror of that is a `Home` pill whose badge is
`'0'` for ever, because nothing is emitting a count under that key.

**Deliberately not an argument for the bump: `SHELL` is unchanged.** No file is added or
renamed by this branch, so there is nothing new to precache and no path a stale `sw.js`
would fail to hold. The bump is entirely about the three files above having to arrive
together.
