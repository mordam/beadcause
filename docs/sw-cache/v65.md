# v65 — an advocate can be turned off from the console (bc-04wd)

The advocate cards on `/monitor` grew a switch: **on** and **off** per workspace, written
through `POST /api/advocate` and held in `advocates.workspaces`. Two files in SHELL move
together for it — `public/monitor.js`, which draws the pair of buttons and the `.adv-why`
line that says who last changed it and when, and `public/style.css`, which is where
`.adv-why` and the switch's two states get their layout.

**A v63 stylesheet under a v64 `monitor.js` is the pair this note is about.** The switch
still works: both buttons post, the daemon obeys, and the reason line renders as readable
text. What is missing is every rule that makes it look like a control — `.adv-why` has no
size, no colour and no spacing, so the sentence explaining why an advocate is off runs
into the card head above it, and the two buttons draw at their unstyled default rather
than as an on/off pair with one of them lit. The page works and looks broken, which is
the "looks like a working page" failure the version exists to prevent.

**A v63 `monitor.js` under a v64 stylesheet is harmless.** The rules are additive; a
console that never draws `.adv-why` simply never matches them, and the advocate cards are
the screen they always were. Nothing on the daemon side is cached at all — `lib/advocate.js`
and the new route in `lib/server.js` are the running build's, whatever the phone holds.

Nothing else in the shell changes. No new path is added, which is why this is a bump for
a mixed pair rather than for a file that would 404.
